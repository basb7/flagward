# Flagward Contributor License Agreement

Thank you for contributing to Flagward.

This agreement is adapted from the [Apache Software Foundation Individual
Contributor License Agreement v2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf),
which is widely used and well understood. It is deliberately not novel: a
contributor should be able to recognise what they are signing.

## Why this exists

Flagward is [MIT licensed](LICENSE) and intended to stay open source.

This agreement does **not** change that, and it does not ask you to give up
ownership of your work. You keep the copyright in everything you write. What
you grant is a licence broad enough that the project can be relicensed later
if it ever has to be — for example to a copyleft licence such as AGPL-3.0, or
to offer a commercial licence alongside the open one.

Without this, that decision becomes impossible rather than merely difficult.
Every contributor owns the copyright in their contribution, so relicensing
requires every one of them to agree. At three contributors that is an
afternoon of email; at fifty, spread over years, with old addresses and
changed jobs, it is a permanent no.

Collecting this now costs a signature. Not collecting it forecloses an
option that cannot be recovered.

**What this is not:** it is not a transfer of ownership, not an exclusive
licence, and not a claim over anything you write outside Flagward. You may
use your own contribution anywhere else, under any terms you like.

## How to sign

Comment on your pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your signature is recorded against your GitHub account and covers every
future contribution to this project. You sign once, not per pull request.

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer, please make sure you have
the right to do so before signing — see clause 4.

## Contributing on behalf of an organisation

If your organisation wants to place several employees' contributions under a
single corporate agreement rather than have each person sign individually,
open an issue and we will arrange one. The clauses above are the individual
form.

## A note on scope

This agreement covers Flagward's server and dashboard. The React SDK
([flagward-sdk-react](https://github.com/basb7/flagward-sdk-react)) carries
its own copy, and is intended to remain permissively licensed regardless of
what the server does — an SDK is linked into its users' own applications, and
a copyleft licence there would reach into code that is not ours to license.
